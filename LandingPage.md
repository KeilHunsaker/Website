## What I Can Help You With

### 1. Legacy Code & Web Maintenance
If your site or application was built years ago and has stopped working—or if your hosting provider is warning you about an outdated PHP version—I can step in and fix it.
* **PHP Upgrades:** Reviewing existing code, identifying deprecated functions, and ensuring scripts run cleanly on modern PHP versions without crashing.
* **Database & Script Fixes:** Diagnosing database connection drops, correcting backend logic errors, and resolving runtime bugs.
* **Code Cleanups:** Organizing repetitive, messy code so your site is stable, faster, and easier to maintain moving forward.

### 2. Contact Forms, Checkouts & Integrations
When customer-facing tools break, you lose inquiries and sales.
* **Broken Contact & Lead Forms:** Resolving delivery failures, script errors, and spam issues so customer messages actually reach your inbox.
* **Payment Gateways:** Repairing or integrating payment processing (such as PayPal) to ensure transactions complete securely.
* **Third-Party APIs:** Connecting or repairing external integrations, such as real-time shipping rate calculators or external data feeds.

### 3. Server, Hosting & DNS Troubleshooting
Control panels and server settings can be confusing, and an incorrect configuration can take your entire site offline.
* **cPanel & Hosting Configuration:** Resolving file permission errors, misconfigured `.htaccess` files, and directory access issues.
* **Domain & DNS Settings:** Correcting MX, SPF, and A records so your domain resolves properly and emails don't end up in spam folders.
* **Site Migrations:** Moving websites and databases cleanly between hosting accounts without downtime or lost data.

### 4. Custom Internal Tools
If your business is struggling with paper forms, messy spreadsheets, or repetitive manual entry, I build lightweight, focused internal tools to handle the routine work.
* **Custom Back-Office Panels:** Built with modern frameworks like Laravel and Filament for fast, secure inventory or order tracking.
* **Workflow Automation:** Replacing manual data calculation with automated invoice generators and reporting tools.

## How I Work

1. **Straightforward Scoping:** You explain what is broken or what you need. I examine the issue and tell you plainly what is causing it, how long it will take to fix, and what it will cost.
2. **Safe & Tested Changes:** Before making updates, files and databases are backed up. Changes are tested to ensure a fix in one area doesn’t break something else.
3. **No Lock-In:** You retain full ownership of your domain, hosting, and accounts. I document what was done so you always understand how your system works.
